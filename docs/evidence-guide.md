# Evidence guide

Prefer official sources, organizational charts, public-health agency or
laboratory pages, official program and governance documents, and approved
internal records. Secondary sources are a fallback.

An evidence record describes a source; referencing it does not prove every
possible assertion. Capture a useful title, publisher, URL or document ID,
retrieval date, locator, and optional SHA-256 content hash.

Entities, relationships, and participations require evidence for release.
Conflicting evidence should produce a disputed or time-bounded assertion for
review, never a silent overwrite.

When resolving a conflict would change identity, retirement, a governed
relationship, or registry policy, preserve the competing evidence and create a
[governance decision record](decisions/README.md).
